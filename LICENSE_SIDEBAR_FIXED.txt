 🎯 THE TWO SUB-MENUS EXPLAINED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1️⃣  License Requests (📋)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    Icon: 📋 FileCheck (blue)
    URL: /licenses/requests
    Purpose: View license APPLICATIONS you submitted
    Shows:
      • Request number (LR-20251110-0001)
      • Title, mining company
      • Quantity, dates
      • Status: DRAFT, SUBMITTED, IN_REVIEW, APPROVED, REJECTED
      • Statistics dashboard
    Your data: ✅ YES! Your submitted request is here


  2️⃣  Issued Licenses (🏆)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    Icon: 🏆 Award (amber/gold)
    URL: /licenses
    Purpose: View ISSUED and ACTIVE licenses
    Shows:
      • License number (EXP-2025-001)
      • Authorized quantity
      • Consumed quantity
      • Expiry dates
      • Quota warnings
    Your data: ❌ EMPTY (no licenses issued yet)


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 🔄 THE WORKFLOW (Why Two Sub-Menus?)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  STEP 1: You Submit Request
  ───────────────────────────────────────
    Action: Fill form, submit
    Saved to: license_requests table
    View on: 📋 License Requests


  STEP 2: Management Reviews
  ───────────────────────────────────────
    Action: Management reviews
    Status: APPROVED or REJECTED
    View on: 📋 License Requests


  STEP 3: License Issued (if approved)
  ───────────────────────────────────────
    Action: Management creates license
    Saved to: licenses table
    View on: 🏆 Issued Licenses


  Right now you're at STEP 1!
