ISSUE: Unit dropdown was empty (not showing g, oz, oz t options)

CAUSE: Select component expects children (option elements), not options prop

FIX APPLIED: ✅


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 🔧 WHAT WAS FIXED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  BEFORE (WRONG):
    <Select
      value={unit}
      onChange={handleUnitChange}
      options={WEIGHT_UNITS.map(...)}  ← Wrong! No options prop exists
    />

  AFTER (FIXED):
    <Select
      value={unit}
      onChange={(e) => handleUnitChange(e.target.value)}
    >
      {WEIGHT_UNITS.map(u => (
        <option key={u.value} value={u.value}>
          {u.abbreviation}
        </option>
      ))}
    </Select>
