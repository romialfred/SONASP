## THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

### Current State Challenges

#### 1. Fragmented Data & Manual Processes

**Problem:**
- Production data recorded in Excel spreadsheets at mining sites
- Shipping manifests created manually in Word documents
- Customs documentation filled out by hand
- Email chains for approvals and confirmations
- Physical document storage and retrieval
- No single source of truth

**Business Impact:**
- Average 4-6 hours per shipment on paperwork alone
- 12-15% error rate in manual data entry
- Lost productivity: 2-3 FTEs per year on redundant data entry
- **Annual Cost: $180,000-$250,000**

#### 2. Inventory Blind Spots

**Problem:**
- Gold in transit not tracked in real-time
- Refinery inventory visibility delayed by 5-7 days
- Available-for-sale balance calculated manually
- Reconciliation between production and inventory requires days
- Cannot commit to sales without time-consuming stock checks

**Business Impact:**
- Opportunity cost: Missing sales due to uncertain inventory
- Working capital inefficiency: Excess safety stock of 15-20%
- Carrying cost: Additional $75,000-$150,000 in working capital
- **Annual Cost: $200,000-$300,000**

#### 3. Compliance & Regulatory Risk

**Problem:**
- Export license quota tracking in spreadsheets
- Manual customs declaration preparation
- Incomplete audit trails
- Document retention challenges
- Difficult to prove compliance during audits
- Risk of export license violations

**Business Impact:**
- Potential fines for quota violations: $50,000-$500,000
- License suspension risk: Loss of export privileges
- Audit preparation costs: 200-300 hours per annual audit
- Insurance premiums: 2-3% higher due to perceived risk
- **Annual Risk Cost: $100,000-$800,000**

#### 4. Pricing & Financial Optimization

**Problem:**
- Gold price captured manually from websites
- FX rate variability not tracked historically
- No mechanism to compare spot vs. forward pricing
- Sales margin analysis done after the fact
- Cannot optimize pricing timing

**Business Impact:**
- Sub-optimal pricing: 2-4% margin erosion
- FX losses: 1-2% on currency conversions
- On $50M annual revenue: $1.5M-$3M potential improvement
- **Annual Cost: $1,500,000-$3,000,000**

#### 5. Stakeholder Coordination

**Problem:**
- Multiple parties (factory, airport, customs, refinery, customer) using different systems
- Email and phone as primary coordination tools
- Status updates delayed by 12-24 hours
- No proactive alerts or notifications
- Difficult to trace accountability

**Business Impact:**
- Shipment delays: 2-3 days average
- Customer service costs: 3-4 FTEs handling inquiries
- Reputation impact: Delayed deliveries
- Lost sales: Customers choosing competitors
- **Annual Cost: $250,000-$400,000**
