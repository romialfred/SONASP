 📊 DURATION CALCULATOR - HOW IT WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Appears when you fill:
    ✓ Start date
    ✓ End date
    ✓ Quantity

  Shows:
    📅 Duration: Total days (end - start + 1)
    📈 Est. Shipments: Based on 3 per week
    📦 Avg per Shipment: Total quantity ÷ shipments

  Example:
    Start: Jan 1, 2025
    End: Jan 31, 2025
    Quantity: 1,500 oz

    Result:
    - Duration: 31 days
    - Est. Shipments: 14 (31 days ÷ 7 × 3/week)
    - Avg per Shipment: 107.14 oz
